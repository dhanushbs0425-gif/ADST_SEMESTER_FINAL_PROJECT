Public Dataset Sources & Citation Details

1. Rice Seed Dataset

Source: Hugging Face (nateraw/rice-image-dataset)

License: CC BY 4.0

Contains 75,000 high-resolution images of 5 Rice seed varieties (Arborio, Basmati, Ipsala, Jasmine, Karacadag).

2. Maize / Corn Grain Dataset

Source: Hugging Face (AdrianaCasadei/CornGrain)

License: CC BY 4.0

Contains annotated YOLO bounding box tray images of Corn seeds categorized into Intact, Skin-damaged, Black-spotted, and Foreign/Broken.

3. Mechanized Soybean Dataset

Source: Hugging Face & Data in Brief (2024)

License: CC BY 4.0

Contains original harvest images and segmentation mask color maps (Green = Normal, Red = Crushed, Yellow = Impurity).
